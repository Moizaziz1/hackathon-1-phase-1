# Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

Welcome to the comprehensive guide on Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics. This book bridges artificial intelligence and embodied robotic systems, guiding learners from simulation to real-world deployment.

## Book Modules

The content is organized into modular sections that can be consumed independently:

### [ROS 2 Fundamentals](./modules/ros2-fundamentals/index.md)
Learn the Robot Operating System for controlling robots. Understand the core concepts, architecture, and tools needed to build robotic applications.

### [Simulation & Digital Twins](./modules/simulation-digital-twins/index.md)
Master simulation environments for testing robotic systems. Learn to create digital twins that accurately represent real-world robots.

### [AI Perception & Navigation](./modules/ai-perception-navigation/index.md)
Understand how robots perceive and navigate their environment. Explore sensors, mapping, and path planning algorithms.

### [Vision-Language-Action Systems](./modules/vision-language-action/index.md)
Explore how robots can understand and respond to human commands. Learn about multimodal AI systems that process vision, language, and execute actions.

### [Capstone Project](./capstone-project/index.md)
Integrate all concepts in a complete humanoid robot system. Apply everything you've learned to build a functional autonomous robot.

## Learning Philosophy

This book follows several core principles:

- **Accuracy First**: All technical explanations are correct and reproducible
- **Learning by Building**: Concepts are taught through hands-on examples and projects
- **Simulation to Reality**: Emphasis on sim-to-real transfer capabilities
- **Open & Accessible**: Content is beginner-friendly while remaining technically deep
- **Modular Knowledge**: Each module stands alone but integrates smoothly with others

## Getting Started

Begin with the [Introduction](./intro.md) to understand the book's structure and approach, then proceed with the module that matches your current knowledge level and interests.
