# Introduction to Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

Welcome to the comprehensive guide on Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics. This book bridges artificial intelligence and embodied robotic systems, guiding learners from simulation to real-world deployment.

## What You'll Learn

This book covers the essential concepts and practical implementations needed to understand and develop humanoid robotic systems:

- **ROS 2 Fundamentals**: Learn the Robot Operating System for controlling robots
- **Simulation & Digital Twins**: Master simulation environments for testing robotic systems
- **AI Perception & Navigation**: Understand how robots perceive and navigate their environment
- **Vision-Language-Action Systems**: Explore how robots can understand and respond to human commands

## Target Audience

This book is designed for:
- AI engineers transitioning into robotics
- Robotics students and researchers
- Software engineers interested in embodied intelligence
- Advanced learners familiar with Python and basic ML concepts

## Learning Approach

Following our core principle of "Learning by Building", each concept is accompanied by hands-on examples and projects. You'll implement what you learn in both simulated and real-world environments, emphasizing sim-to-real transfer throughout.

## Book Structure

The book is organized into modular sections that can be consumed independently while building upon each other:

1. **ROS 2 Fundamentals** - Core concepts and tools for robotic systems
2. **Simulation & Digital Twins** - Creating and using simulation environments
3. **AI Perception & Navigation** - Understanding environments and moving through them
4. **Vision-Language-Action Systems** - Processing human commands and executing tasks
5. **Capstone Project** - Integrating all concepts in a complete humanoid robot system

Each module includes theoretical concepts, practical examples, and hands-on labs to reinforce your learning.
